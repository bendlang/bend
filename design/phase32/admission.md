# Candidate03 selection and accepted costs

Select checked03 for release **only after** the frozen broad gates, canonical
source audit, installation verification and 42 ordinary/relocated CLI controls
close successfully. The Phase31 installed compiler remains available until that
point. This records the selection decision after the measurements; it does not
change the earlier experimental criteria or turn an unfinished gate into a pass.

The local criterion passes: every checked increment has disjoint improvement
ranges on the complete pair and independent fold. The selected output improves
the original four-pair edit-distance program 3.54× in the controlled comparison,
reducing its TypeScript gap from 14.49× to 4.09×. Original Mandelbrot/RLE bytes
are unchanged. All three regression-canary timing ranges overlap.

Explicitly accept the measured **36.75 ms / 1.91% increase in Mandelbrot library
compilation**, whose request-time ranges are disjoint. Its baseline median is
1,921.46 ms and candidate median 1,958.21 ms. Edit-distance compilation's −0.45%
overlaps and is not an established improvement. Compiler-request performance
therefore remains a separate unresolved gap; this is a generated-program gain.
The repeated stop-list query and other cache prototypes are not promoted to
offset this cost, because their respective safety or speed criteria failed.

Accept 57 additional physical Bend lines (+0.335%), six functions and two private
plan tags, with no new datatype or module. The three mechanisms reuse the existing
closed-region proof and guards. Pair/fold emitted modules grow 4.21%/1.08%, mainly
from bridge bodies. Candidate Mandelbrot compilation's peak-RSS median rises
about 0.39%; the controlled acquisition remains well below the enforced budget.
The [exact measurements](../../implementation/phase32/final-measurements/measurements.md)
and [complexity accounting](../../implementation/phase32/local-complexity.md)
retain the full figures and scope.

The tradeoff is justified by the demonstrated original-program gain, the small
source/cost increase, unchanged public representation and runtime, and the
independent semantic controls. Preserve all observations, including remaining
warmup drift and the rejected private checker/cache experiments. Do not infer
general backend, GPU, proof-kernel or self-emitted fixed-point conformance from
this selection. Future performance comparisons start from the admitted candidate
and retain these costs as part of their baseline.
