# Constructor guard: immutable input and raw demand controls

Prospective clarification approved by root before the direct controls or checked candidate run. The production helper consumes compiler-owned immutable KDef graphs. Its new child-list match may read an already-demanded nonempty list tag again before delegating to the existing recursive lookup. Such repeated reads are not a new language-level semantic demand on an immutable tag. Accessing a previously unused child, definition field or suffix is a different issue and remains a blocker.

Preserve literal raw getter logs, including a tag getter that throws on its second read. Do not label a differing raw getter sequence exact or make an arbitrary effectful-getter equivalence claim. Separate the semantic gate: identical pure graph winner/reference, first error on initially demanded malformed fields, preorder and no new previously unread field demands. The independent controller review must record which raw cases pass or differ and why. No larger production worker is justified solely to equalize observation counts on effectful JavaScript getters. Existing design/source01 remain immutable; this addendum narrows interpretation rather than altering fixtures after observing results.
