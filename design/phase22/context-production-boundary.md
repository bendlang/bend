# Contextual parser production boundary

The Phase21 source is the only project parent. Reuse Phase19 cursor/contextual
changes by an explicit three-way front-file transfer against their Phase17
ancestor, retaining all Phase20 declaration and Phase21 range corrections.
Do not copy the old whole project, checker, host exports or old compiler output.

The first preparation is a staging barrier, not a completion claim. Stage4
explicitly lacks lambda/All, annotation, parallel, do, template/operator and
marked expression owners. Enabling its private body entry and falling back on
Unsupported would duplicate parsing and select errors incorrectly. Keep one
grammar and migrate its actual recursive checkpoints before the production
declaration-body entry changes. Remove fixed name-state probe protocols and
private Parsed/Error/Unsupported public entry wrappers from the prospective
production module. No private-only capability is the final milestone.

The production seed must combine the existing dependency namespace/alias scope,
the completed same-module declarations, current signature and actual opened
header parameters. Preserve fresh identity and outer lexical restoration. A
completed body must not run through the old binder-resolving traversal again.
Use explicit completion transport; never infer it from source ranges. The final
route must lower groups at their actual boundary and retain Body rows until
their enclosing block completes. Reuse ff_flat and shallow pattern validators.

Retirement targets are the fixed research probe APIs immediately, then the
deferred local/row pattern-and-scope responsibility after production migration,
and the duplicate f_flat family once all callers use the stateful flatten owner.
Shared row/substitution helpers stay. A function-level caller census must justify
actual deletions; no whole-module or50% deletion estimate is claimed.

The alternative error-retaining raw AST would still need ordered failure
transport at every reached grammar continuation, plus a real completed-group
boundary, while making the delayed scoper responsible for parse cursor/fresh
effects. Investigate its exact cost before substituting it for contextual
migration; a wrapper or immediate comma error alone is disproved by saved C1.

The first30-minute checkpoint reports actual prepared/checked boundaries and
remaining owners. It is not an end-of-work time budget. Genuine checked B1,
maintained36, original196 and C1 remain strict evidence. No production promotion
until the actual public route passes its semantic and resource gates.
