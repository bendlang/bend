# General tail-Let promotion criterion

Freeze this decision rule before the first screen. A short screen only selects
follow-up work. Require a long confirmation with at least a 10% median
improvement in one declared workload, disjoint sample ranges and timed-half
changes normally below 5%; do not promote a confirmed regression greater than
3% in the other workload. If a screen is promising but drifting, retain it and
use the existing longer-warmup runner.

A whole-Mandelbrot win alone may come from private Lets already changed in
attempt14. Require a new isolated comparison against checked attempt14 before
attributing that benefit to extending statement emission into generic
callbacks. Production additionally requires focused semantic gates and genuine
checked output; a fast disposable rewrite is insufficient.

The complete original editdist result is an untimed correctness gate. It is
not in the first timing screen, whose cases are the complete owned row32 state
and original Mandelbrot bench(2,0). Keep both scopes explicit.
