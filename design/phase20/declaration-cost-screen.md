# Final declaration candidate cost screen

After the constructor punctuation correction and combined semantic gates close,
measure the final Phase20 checked derivative against the installed Phase19 live
checker and the unchanged pinned TypeScript compiler. This supplements the
integration plan: it tests for an accidental checking-cost regression, without
assuming a speedup from grammar corrections.

Use the unchanged phase16/check-matrix-v2.mjs runner, identical final candidate
assembled source, serial TS/B/C/C/B/TS order, fresh processes on CPU0, 4 MiB stack
and 4 GiB heap. Review the complete frozen host membership and require unchanged
host, Base, runtime, pin and version5 transformation. Each Bend image uses its
own validated Base cache; TypeScript checks Base. Do not flush OS caches or mix
this window with historical ratios. Other compiler/probe/hash/archive jobs must
be closed or held throughout all six measurements. Freeze readiness and exact
source/API/tool/input identities before running.

All observations must pass their ordinary type/trust contract, agree on the
unsafe definition set and retain their process/request timing and peak RSS.
Investigate a process slowdown exceeding 5% before promotion. Smaller changes
are a cost screen with two samples per image, not a statistical performance
claim. Preserve failures or ambiguous results; no selective removal of samples.
No program emission or generated-code performance is measured.
